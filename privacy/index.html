Privacy policy

Effective 21 August 2026

This policy explains how CatchFolio handles information. CatchFolio is designed as a local-first fishing record and does not require an account.

Information you enter

Trips, zero-catch outcomes, catches, custom species, size, kept or released state, bait or lure, technique, private spot labels, manually entered coordinates, tackle, maintenance dates, notes, preferences and local reminder schedules are stored on your device. CatchFolio does not operate a developer cloud database for these records.

CatchFolio does not request camera, photo/media library, precise location or coarse location permission. Coordinates, if you choose to save them, are typed by you. Spots are not published or shared automatically.

Exports and restore

You can initiate CSV, readable text and JSON exports. An export leaves CatchFolio only when you choose a destination through your device. The selected destination may handle that copy under its own privacy terms. Restore validates CatchFolio backup structure before changing the local record set.

Advertising and consent

CatchFolio uses Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform. Where required, the consent flow is completed before an ad request. Depending on your device, region, choices and Google's current services, Google may process an IP address and approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, ads viewed, app or ad interactions, crash information and performance information for advertising, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention.

CatchFolio disables Google's publisher first-party identifier setting in the app. For more information, read Google's privacy policy and Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure guidance.

App update delivery

CatchFolio may use Expo application update services to deliver compatible app updates. Update requests may process technical information such as operating system, the developer project identifier, a random installation token and update diagnostics for app functionality and update delivery. See Expo's privacy policy.

Notifications

Reminders are optional and scheduled locally on the device. CatchFolio does not register a remote push-notification token.

Deleting your information

Delete individual records inside CatchFolio, clear the app's device data, or uninstall the app to remove its local records. Copies you exported must be deleted from their chosen destinations separately.

Security and scope

No storage method is risk-free. Keep your device protected and treat private spot coordinates as sensitive. CatchFolio does not provide navigation, public spots, legal rules, species identification, forecasts or safety advice.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app or its service providers change. The effective date above will be revised when a material update is published.
